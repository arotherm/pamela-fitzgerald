What Pamela offers

Sessions

Four ways to work with Pamela. Each session is unhurried and one-to-one.

The headline offering

Equine Reiki with horses

Reiki in the company of horses. The session takes place outdoors, in the field, with the horses free to come close or keep their distance. You bring nothing but yourself. No riding, and no horse experience needed — the work is non-mounted, and the horses are sentient partners in it, not tools.

Private sessions explore presence, energetic attunement and horse-led reflection, with the horses at liberty. Pamela also offers compassionate horse care and stewardship practice, and Learn to Love Horses — a gentle introduction for littles.

In the field, with the horses.

Regular Reiki

A quiet, hands-on Reiki session. Time set aside to slow down, rest, and be still — in the clinic in Midland. Sessions run 75 to 120 minutes, lying comfortably or seated, with hands resting lightly on or just above the body. Nothing to guide or analyse — your system leads at its own pace.

In the clinic, in Midland.

Embodied Path sessions

Somatic work that brings attention back to the body — breath, posture, and the ground under your feet. Offered across the region. Each session is 1½ hours, in two forms: Embodied Presence, a restorative practice of Reiki, somatic awareness and intentional presence; and Elemental Presence, the same practice deepened with the grounding warmth of heated volcanic stones.

Tiny, Soul Set Studio on Yonge Street in Midland, and Wyebridge.

Online sessions

Reiki and Embodied Path work from wherever you are, by video. A quiet hour, no travel needed.

About Pamela

Pamela Fitzgerald

Somatic Specialist & Reiki Practitioner · Nurturing Roots Therapy

Pamela works with Reiki and somatic practice in Midland, Ontario — with people, and with horses. Her work is calm, unhurried and practical: time set aside, in a quiet place, with someone who takes the work seriously.

She is a Neuromuscular Rehabilitation Specialist, Equine Assisted Learning Certified Facilitator, Registered Yoga Teacher (RYT 500), Compassionate Horse Care and Horse Behaviour Specialist, and Reiki Practitioner. She spent years working in horse rescue and now partners with her own small herd. Her work bridges human movement, nervous system awareness, and the wisdom of horses.

She offers sessions in the field with horses, in the clinic in Midland, and online.

To talk about a session, write to pamela@pamelafitzgerald.ca or call 705-528-9240.
